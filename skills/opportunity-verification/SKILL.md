# Skill: Opportunity Verification and Publication

## Goal

Ensure no opportunity is publicly represented as verified without
evidence for the specific company and opportunity.

## Required checks

1.  Establish company legal identity using reliable sources.
2.  Confirm official contact channel and representative authority.
3.  Confirm authorization to offer the specific product/opportunity
    where such authorization is relevant.
4.  Confirm current opportunity status and territory availability.
5.  Capture cost components, fee payer, source and date; unknowns stay
    unknown.
6.  Record eligibility, infrastructure, licence and documentation
    requirements.
7.  Review application route, agreement/contract and material caveats.
8.  Record evidence references, reviewer, decision, review date and
    expiry/re-review trigger.
9.  Resolve contradictions or escalate them; do not publish on
    unresolved material contradictions.
10. Obtain authorized reviewer approval before publication.

## Publication states

Use explicit internal states such as draft, pending review, approved,
review due, suspended and rejected. Public labels should be accurate and
should not reveal sensitive internal details.

## Re-review and suspension

Re-review on expiry, provider change, complaint, suspected fraud,
territory change, stale fees or other material change. Suspend/unpublish
while a material concern is investigated.

## Communication rules

-   Distinguish company-provided statements from independently checked
    facts.
-   Never claim that verification guarantees profitability, safety,
    approval or success.
-   Never invent evidence, official relationships, investment values or
    availability.
-   Do not use AI output as the sole approval.
-   Keep evidence access restricted and log sensitive access.

## Output

Provide decision, evidence summary, unresolved issues, reviewer and
date, validity/review date, public claims permitted and restrictions. If
evidence is insufficient, return for more information or reject; do not
publish.
