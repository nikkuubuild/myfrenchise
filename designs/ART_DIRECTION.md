# Franchise Dekho --- Art Direction

**Direction:** Editorial sophistication + enterprise credibility\
**Purpose:** Design brief for the first prototype; not a completed
design or production stylesheet.

## Brand character

-   Informed, composed, pragmatic and trustworthy.
-   Indian business context without clichéd stock imagery or decorative
    motifs.
-   Premium through craft and clarity, not luxury clichés.
-   Approachable to first-time entrepreneurs while credible to
    experienced operators.

## Visual references to seek

-   Editorial business publications: typography, grid and pacing.
-   Consulting research reports: clarity, evidence and structured
    information.
-   Well-designed financial/service products: precise forms, data
    hierarchy and interaction states.
-   Real Indian retail, manufacturing, food, service and distribution
    environments.

Do not copy any reference site. Use references to discuss principles,
not reproduce a layout.

## Proposed palette

-   Ink navy `#172B3A`: primary text, navigation and structure.
-   Warm paper `#F6F3ED`: optional editorial surface.
-   Muted green `#49685B`: restrained accent.
-   Soft brass `#D7B77A`: occasional highlight only.
-   Define neutral text, borders, surface, success, warning, error and
    information tokens before implementation.
-   Check WCAG contrast for every actual foreground/background pairing.
    Accent colours are not automatically suitable for small text or
    buttons.

## Composition

-   Use a strong typographic hero with one clear primary action.
-   Introduce asymmetry only when it improves narrative or scanning.
-   Alternate dense data sections with readable editorial content.
-   Use open sections, hairline dividers and restrained backgrounds.
-   Reserve cards for content that is genuinely a discrete item or
    action.
-   Avoid excessive full-width section colour changes.

## Image direction

-   Prefer authentic, relevant photography with appropriate usage
    rights.
-   Show real business contexts, people and environments where possible.
-   Avoid images that imply a company relationship, endorsement or
    verified partnership that does not exist.
-   Provide meaningful alt text for informative images; decorative
    images should have empty alt text in implementation.
-   Optimize formats and dimensions for performance.

## Typography brief

Select fonts only after testing Latin and Devanagari coverage,
readability, weight range, licensing and performance. Establish: -
Display headline scale. - Page title and section title scale. - Body and
long-form scale. - Labels, metadata and tabular numerals. - Line-height
and line-length rules. Avoid ultra-light fonts, very small metadata and
excessive all-caps.

## First prototype screens

1.  Homepage: proposition + discovery entry point.
2.  Opportunity search: filters + results + clear metadata.
3.  Opportunity detail: evidence, cost, eligibility, location and
    action.
4.  Managed-service enquiry: progressive form.
5.  Customer case overview: status, next action and timeline.
6.  Admin verification queue: operational table and review action.

## Prototype evaluation

Test with at least a small set of representative users before treating
the design as final. Ask users to: - Explain what Franchise Dekho
does. - Find a suitable opportunity within a stated budget/location. -
Identify what is verified and what remains uncertain. - Find the full
fee/refund terms. - Explain the next step and who makes the final
approval decision.

Record issues and update the design. Do not use this document as a claim
that user testing has already happened.
