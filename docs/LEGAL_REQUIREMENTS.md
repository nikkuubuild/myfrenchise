# Legal, Policy and Compliance Workstream

**Important:** This is a planning inventory, not legal advice, and does
not certify compliance. Obtain review from qualified Indian counsel and
relevant sector experts before accepting customers, collecting sensitive
documents or charging fees.

## 1. Public-facing policies to prepare

-   Terms of Use.
-   Privacy Notice.
-   Cookie Notice and preference controls.
-   Managed Services Agreement / scope of service.
-   Fees, Billing, Cancellation and Refund Policy.
-   Opportunity Verification and Publication Policy.
-   Opportunity and Earnings Disclaimer.
-   Company/Partner Terms.
-   Fraud Reporting and Grievance Redressal.
-   Acceptable Use and content rules.
-   OTP and electronic communications notice.
-   Document upload, access, sharing and retention notice.
-   Payment and invoice terms.
-   Any sector-specific disclosures required for the actual services.

## 2. Legal review areas

Counsel should assess applicability and current status of: - Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules/commencement
notifications. - Information Technology Act and applicable rules. -
Consumer Protection Act and applicable e-commerce/advertising rules,
where relevant. - Telecom/DLT and commercial messaging requirements for
OTP and SMS. - GST, invoicing, accounting and refund obligations. -
Payment gateway and payment-system requirements. - Sector-specific
requirements for banking/Business Correspondent services, insurance,
lending, financial products, healthcare, education or other regulated
opportunities. - Contract enforceability, electronic records, grievance
handling and limitation/disclaimer wording. - Data transfers,
processor/vendor contracts and breach response.

Applicability depends on actual service model and the law in force at
launch; do not infer that one general checklist satisfies every
obligation.

## 3. Disclosure principles

-   Identify the contracting party and service provider clearly.
-   Explain what Franchise Dekho does and does not do.
-   Identify who pays the platform and any material commercial
    relationship with an opportunity provider.
-   Show all applicable fees, taxes, inclusions, exclusions and refund
    terms before payment.
-   Do not claim guaranteed approval, funding, earnings, profitability
    or success.
-   Explain that the opportunity provider retains final decision-making
    authority.
-   Distinguish verified facts from provider-supplied claims and
    disclose meaningful unknowns.
-   Describe how to report suspected fraud and how complaints are
    handled.

## 4. Privacy and consent

-   Publish a clear privacy notice before collecting personal data.
-   Identify purposes, data categories, recipients, retention approach
    and contact/grievance channel.
-   Collect only necessary data.
-   Record appropriate consent/authorization for sharing customer
    documents and submitting applications.
-   Make optional marketing consent separate from service processing
    where required.
-   Provide applicable rights/withdrawal mechanisms and a documented
    request workflow.
-   Establish vendor contracts and access controls.
-   Define retention/deletion and incident response with counsel.

## 5. Publication and evidence governance

-   Maintain a written verification checklist and reviewer decision.
-   Store source/provenance and last-checked date.
-   Define expiry/re-review periods and suspension triggers.
-   Keep sensitive evidence internal.
-   Provide a correction/dispute route for companies and customers.
-   Ensure public badges match the actual scope of verification.

## 6. Payment and cancellation

Before payment, present amount, taxes, payer, service deliverables,
dependencies, timing assumptions, cancellation/refund rules and any
non-refundable components with legal review. Record payment provider
references and refunds. Do not promise outcomes controlled by third
parties.

## 7. Launch gate

No public launch until counsel has reviewed the actual operating model
and required policies, relevant provider/sector permissions have been
assessed, customer support/grievance ownership is assigned, and
payment/consent flows match the published terms.
